Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Who we are

SmartStart is a home-education tutoring service run by Ben Mottershead. You can contact us via WhatsApp on the Contact page.

What data we collect

This website does not collect any personal data through forms, cookies, or tracking tools. We do not use Google Analytics or any other analytics service.

If you contact us via WhatsApp, any information you share is handled through WhatsApp's own platform and privacy policy.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or any local storage to track you.

Third-party links

Our site contains links to external websites (such as Amazon and Gov.uk). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Your rights

As we collect no personal data through this website, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. If you have any questions, contact us via the Contact page.

Lesson recordings

All lessons are recorded, by attending these zoom lessons you are agreeing for your child to be recorded, only to be shared privately to the private WhatsApp group that is controlled and monitored regularly. The recordings are strictly limited to active class members only and are not allowed to be shared. Cameras are not enforced, only encouraged.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
